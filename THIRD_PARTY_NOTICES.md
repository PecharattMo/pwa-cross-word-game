English headwords and Thai translations were adapted from LEXiTRON 2.0, developed by the National Electronics and Computer Technology Center (NECTEC).

Required acknowledgement:

Source: <https://opend-portal.nectec.or.th/dataset/lexitron-2-0>

## Open English WordNet 2025

English definitions were adapted from Open English WordNet 2025, released by the Open English WordNet community under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Open English WordNet is derived from Princeton WordNet.

- Project and downloads: <https://en-word.net/>
- License: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>
- Princeton WordNet: <https://wordnet.princeton.edu/>

The generated catalog is `src/data/vocabulary-library.json`. The reproducible filtering script is `scripts/build_vocabulary_library.py`.
